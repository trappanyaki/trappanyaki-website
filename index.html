BEFORE YOU ORDER

FAQ

What does the 8-plate cap actually mean?

Every drop is capped at eight plates so nothing sits. When the eighth slot is claimed, that batch closes and the next window opens. It keeps the sear tight and the lid going on hot.

Where do I pick up?

Tracy, CA. Your exact pickup pin goes out with the confirmation once the batch locks. Pickup only — no delivery right now.

What's in the crossed drizzle?

Two passes: a chipotle-orange mayo laid across a dark soy glaze, crossed so you get both in one bite. Fresh scallion goes on after the heat. Sauce on the side on request.

Can you cater a private or corporate event?

Yes — full mobile setup, on-site flat-top, custom multi-course menus, and the live teppanyaki show. Email trappanyakillc@gmail.com with your date, headcount and location.

Do you handle allergies or dietary swaps?

Tell us in the order notes. We can go veg-only, skip the mayo, or run sauce-on-the-side. Everything shares one flat-top, so we can't promise a fully allergen-free tray.

How far ahead do drops get posted?

The night before. Slots move fast once the batch goes live — build your batch here first so you can send it through Square the second it opens.
